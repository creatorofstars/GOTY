# GOTY — THE LAST STAR

> **Genre:** Fantasy Adventure + Action + Light Comedy

> **One Star Fragment. Two kingdoms. Only one can survive.**

---

## 🌟 The Story

Long ago, the **Heart Star** protected the world with its magical light.
Every spell, every forest, every creature drew life from it.

But the Heart Star is dying.

Its light is fading — and without it, the kingdoms will slowly disappear.

There is only one hope: the ancient **Star Fragment**, the only artifact capable of restoring the Heart Star.

But the Fragment holds a terrible limitation:

> **It can save only one kingdom.**

When the truth is discovered, two young warriors are chosen — one from each kingdom.
Both travel to the ancient temple where the Fragment sleeps.
Both want to save their home.

The temple has one rule, older than the kingdoms themselves:

> **Only the strongest warrior may carry the Star Fragment.**

This is not a ceremony. It is a life-or-death trial.

**There can only be one winner.**

## ⚔️ Character: The Warriors

Two warriors enter the temple's arena. Only one walks out with the Fragment. Select your warrior

### 🏹 The Archer 

A calm forest ranger who grew up protecting her kingdom's borders.

- **Strength:** precision, long-range attacks
- **Personality:** calm, confident, focused

> "I don't miss."

### 🔫 The Arcane Gunner 

A young inventor who built a magical firearm powered by crystals.

- **Strength:** fast attacks, technology combined with magic
- **Personality:** inventive, confident, slightly arrogant

> "Why use a sword when technology exists?"

### 💣 The Bomb Mage 

A reckless magician who specializes in explosive magic.

- **Strength:** powerful, unpredictable attacks
- **Personality:** chaotic, overconfident, funny

> "I know exactly what I'm doing."

*(Narrator: they absolutely do not.)*

### 🥷 The Shadow Ninja 

A mysterious warrior from the Shadow Clan.

- **Strength:** speed, evasion, surprise attacks
- **Personality:** mysterious, quiet, confident

> "You never saw me coming."

## 📖 Story Progression

1. **The Heart Star** — its light is fading.
2. **The Threat** — without the Heart Star, the kingdoms will slowly disappear.
3. **The Star Fragment** — it is discovered as the only artifact capable of restoring the Heart Star.
4. **The Limitation** — the Fragment can save only one kingdom.
5. **The Ancient Temple** — two warriors from different kingdoms arrive.
6. **The Trial** — the temple declares: only the strongest warrior may claim the Star Fragment.
7. **The Battle** — the two warriors fight in the temple's arena.
8. **The Result** — one warrior wins and claims the Star Fragment. The winning kingdom is saved. The losing kingdom disappears.
